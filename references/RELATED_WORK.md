# Related work and priority boundary

- John Watrous, *The Theory of Quantum Information* (2018), [book](https://jhwatrous.github.io/TQI.pdf): foundational channels, instruments, trace-distance discrimination and symmetric tensors; educational anchor only.
- *No Cloning of Quantum Ensembles* (Du and collaborators): the direct operational predecessor for cloning labeled ensembles. Verify authors, version, theorem statement, and quantitative scope against the primary paper before claiming novelty.
- [Quantum-Conditional-Learning](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Quantum-Conditional-Learning): reproduces a conditional stabilizer branch after a held-out label, not an entire distribution of doubled trajectories.
- [Quantum-Coherent-Collisions](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Quantum-Coherent-Collisions): coherent Hadamard-power extraction from unknown copies, with a different output and collision resource law.

The research contribution sought here is **not** the generic impossibility of cloning unknown quantum states. It is a quantitative comparison between supplying a probability assignment and supplying only its multiset for the same physical output. The current repository has not completed the two universal bounds required to establish that separation.
